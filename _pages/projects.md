## <i class="fas fa-chart-line section-icon"></i> Forecasting CO₂ Emissions in Real Estate
<p class="entry-meta">In collaboration with BNP Paribas, France</p>

I chose to participate in this project alongside my Machine Learning course during my first year of master's studies, as it provided a concrete and applied setting in which to implement the concepts learned in class. The work lasted three months, with weekly meetings with data scientists from BNP Paribas. Conducted in a four-person team, the focus was on the study of time series in order to forecast future CO₂ emissions by real estate property category. Tasks included data preprocessing, feature engineering, training models such as ARIMA, LSTM and Prophet, and performance benchmarking.

## <i class="fas fa-wifi section-icon"></i> Optimization of an electromagnetic anechoic chamber
<p class="entry-meta">MICS laboratory, CentraleSupélec, France</p>

Given a wave propagation PDE model in a chamber, the goal was to minimize the energy of an electromagnetic wave inside the chamber by determining the optimal distribution of absorbing material. The work involved both the theoretical study of the model (well-posedness of the PDE system) and the implementation of numerical algorithms — finite elements for solving the PDEs, and gradient descent in Hilbert spaces to determine the optimal distribution of absorbing material.

## <i class="fas fa-share-alt section-icon"></i> Viral Marketing Campaign
<p class="entry-meta">In collaboration with Artefact, France</p>

As part of a one-month project with the data and AI consultancy Artefact, I integrated a four-person team to analyze the viral dynamics of an online climate-awareness campaign created for Greenpeace. The campaign involved a fictional real-estate startup ("Orizon") promoting seaside homes projected for the year 2100, using IPCC and NASA climate data. Our work consisted in studying how information spread across social networks, identifying key influencers, modeling diffusion patterns, and proposing data-driven strategies to increase the campaign's reach and impact. The project combined elements of graph analysis, social-network modeling, and practical decision-making for digital marketing.

## <i class="fas fa-shield-alt section-icon"></i> Detection of anomalies in an IoT network
<p class="entry-meta">In collaboration with Bouygues Télécom, France</p>

In a five-person team, I conducted an analysis of a log dataset following a DDoS attack to identify the characteristics of the attack. This project was carried out as a conclusion to the Information Theory / IoT course I followed during my first year of studies at CentraleSupélec. Tasks included developing machine learning tools for the detection of streaming DDoS attacks.
