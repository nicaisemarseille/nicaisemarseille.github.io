      <span class="entry-title">Oral Examiner (“Colleur”) — Classes Préparatoires</span>
      <p>Alongside my internship at INRIA, I also served as an oral examiner in second-year <em>Classes Préparatoires</em> (France's selective two-year post-secondary programs preparing students for competitive entrance exams to top engineering schools), at Lycée International de Valbonne. My role consisted of conducting weekly oral mathematics examinations (<em>colles</em>) with small groups of students, assessing their problem-solving skills, guiding them through advanced exercises, and providing detailed feedback on their reasoning and methods.</p>
